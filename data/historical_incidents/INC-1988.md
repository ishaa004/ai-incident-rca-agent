# INC-1988: checkout-service errors during processor outage

Date: 2026-01-22
Services: checkout-service, payment-gateway

## Summary

External card processor experienced a partial outage. payment-gateway
`processor.charge` p99 latency rose to 25s+. checkout-service, which called
payment-gateway with a 30s timeout at the time, began timing out user
requests and returning 5xx after roughly 25-30s of hung requests, causing
thread pool saturation on checkout-service itself as a secondary effect.

## Root cause

Primary: external card processor outage (not owned by us). Secondary,
self-inflicted: checkout-service's timeout for payment-gateway calls (30s)
was far too long, so instead of failing fast, requests piled up and
saturated checkout-service's own request-handling threads, causing
unrelated endpoints (not just checkout) to degrade.

## Resolution

Processor outage resolved after 22 minutes. Follow-up: reduced
`PAYMENT_GATEWAY_TIMEOUT_MS` from 30000 to 2000 and added a circuit breaker
so a slow/erroring payment-gateway fails fast instead of saturating
checkout-service's thread pool.

## Lessons

- A slow (not erroring) downstream dependency can be worse than a hard-down
  one if timeouts are too generous — it saturates caller resources.
- Circuit breakers should be added for every synchronous downstream call,
  not just ones that have historically failed.
